Overview

Srijan Spacia by Srijan Realty, Madhyamgram

Srijan Spacia is a 3.6-acre residential and retail development with 449 flats across six G+12 towers on Jessore Road, right beside Reliance Mall and before Madhyamgram Chowmatha. Almost every home is 3-side open, and the community has 64% open space including podium, an 18,000 sq.ft. clubhouse and 29,000+ sq.ft. of retail.

  • 64% open space, including podium
  • 3 BHK and 4 BHK flats with three-side-open layouts
  • 18,000 sq.ft. clubhouse
  • 29,000+ sq.ft. of retail
  • 477 parking spaces: 99 basement, 334 covered and 44 mechanical
  • Developer-advertised possession target: 2030
  • Registered WBRERA completion date: 31 December 2032

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers about Spacia flats in Madhyamgram.

What is Srijan Spacia?

Srijan Spacia (SPACIA by Srijan Realty) is a residential and retail development of 3 and 4 BHK flats in Madhyamgram, Kolkata, with 449 apartments across six G+12 towers on 3.6 acres.

Where is Srijan Spacia located?

Srijan Spacia is at Holding No 28, Jessore Road South, Mouza Doharia, Ward 09, beside Reliance Mall, Madhyamgram, Kolkata 700129 – on Jessore Road, before Madhyamgram Chowmatha.

What is the price of 3 BHK flats in Srijan Spacia?

Srijan Realty’s current official price list starts 3 BHK + 2T flats at ₹81.30 Lacs* and 3 BHK + 3T flats at ₹1.03 Cr*. Prices are subject to change; confirm the current quote and availability with the sales team.

What is the price of 4 BHK flats in Srijan Spacia?

4 BHK + 3T flats start at ₹1.19 Cr* according to Srijan Realty’s current official price list. Prices are subject to change; confirm the current quote with the sales team.

What are the configurations and carpet areas?

Srijan Spacia has 3 BHK + 2T, 3 BHK + 3T and 4 BHK + 3T flats. Official tower charts show respective Flat Carpet Area ranges of 703–844, 930–952 and 1,038–1,085 sq.ft.; corresponding Super Built-up Area ranges are 1,079–1,298, 1,395–1,422 and 1,619–1,693 sq.ft.

How far is Srijan Spacia from the airport?

Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport is approximately 5.9 km from Srijan Spacia, and the upcoming Jai Hind Airport Metro station is also about 5.9 km away. The official brochure describes the airport as about a 15-minute straight drive along Jessore Road. Distances are approximate.

Which metro and railway stations are near Srijan Spacia?

The official brochure lists the upcoming New Barrackpore Metro Station at approximately 1.8 km, Madhyamgram Railway Station at 2 km and Dum Dum Railway & Metro Station at 11.7 km. New Town is approximately 14.8 km and Salt Lake Sector V approximately 17.7 km away.

What is near Srijan Spacia?

Reliance Mall is approximately 50 m away, with Spacia’s own retail arena in-house. The brochure lists schools from approximately 200 m to 2.7 km and hospitals from approximately 300 m to 2.3 km; distances are approximate.

What is Srijan Spacia’s RERA number?

Srijan Spacia is registered with the West Bengal Real Estate Regulatory Authority (WBRERA) under registration number WBRERA/P/NOR/2025/003059. You can verify the project record on the WBRERA website.

What is the possession date for Srijan Spacia?

Srijan Realty advertises 2030 as the possession target. WBRERA records 31 December 2032 as the registered completion date. Confirm current timelines with Srijan Realty.

What amenities does Srijan Spacia have?

Project facilities include an 18,000 sq.ft. clubhouse, 29,000+ sq.ft. of retail, landscaped spaces, a swimming pool, an outdoor gym, a butterfly garden and a community hall. Srijan Spacia is IGBC Platinum Pre-Certified.

Are Srijan Spacia flats 3-side open?

Yes. The official brochure says almost all Srijan Spacia apartments are 3-side open with no shared walls, so homes get light and cross-ventilation from three sides.
