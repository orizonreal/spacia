Where is SPACIA by Srijan Realty located?

SPACIA is on Jessore Road near Madhyamgram Chowmatha in Madhyamgram, West Bengal.

How many homes and towers are planned at SPACIA?

The project comprises 449 apartments across six G+12 towers.

What apartment configurations are listed for SPACIA?

SPACIA offers 3 and 4 BHK homes. The supplied floor plan area charts include Tower 1 and Tower 2 configurations; the configuration table shows the area figures by tower.

What open space, parking and shared facilities are listed?

The project lists 64% open space and 477 parking spaces: 99 basement, 334 covered and 44 mechanical. Its facilities include an 18,000 sq.ft. clubhouse and more than 29,000 sq.ft. of retail.

What is SPACIA’s WBRERA registration number?
