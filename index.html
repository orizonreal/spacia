OVERVIEW

SPACIA by Srijan Realty at Madhyamgram

SPACIA is a 3.6-acre residential and retail development with 449 apartments across six G+12 towers. It has 64% open space including podium, 3-side-open homes, an 18,000 sq.ft. clubhouse and 29,000+ sq.ft. of retail.

  • 64% open space, including podium
  • 3 and 4 BHK homes, with three-side-open layouts
  • 18,000 sq.ft. clubhouse
  • 29,000+ sq.ft. of retail
  • 477 parking spaces: 99 basement, 334 covered and 44 mechanical
  • Developer-advertised possession target: 2030
  • Registered WBRERA completion date: 31 December 2032

SPACIA Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers about SPACIA by Srijan Realty.

What is SPACIA?

SPACIA by Srijan Realty is a 3 and 4 BHK residential and retail development in Madhyamgram, Kolkata, with 449 apartments across six G+12 towers on 3.6 acres.

Where is SPACIA located?

SPACIA is at Jessore Road South, Mouza Doharia, Ward 09, beside Reliance Mall, Madhyamgram, Kolkata 700129.

What is SPACIA’s RERA number?

SPACIA is registered as WBRERA/P/NOR/2025/003059.

What are the configurations and published areas?

SPACIA has 3 BHK + 2T, 3 BHK + 3T and 4 BHK + 3T homes. Official tower charts show respective Flat Carpet Area ranges of 703–844, 930–952 and 1,038–1,085 sq.ft.; corresponding Super Built-up Area ranges are 1,079–1,298, 1,395–1,422 and 1,619–1,693 sq.ft. The table shows each published tower range.

What is the starting price?

The official page currently lists 3 BHK + 2T from ₹81.30 Lacs, 3 BHK + 3T from ₹1.03 Cr and 4 BHK + 3T from ₹1.19 Cr. Prices are subject to change; confirm the current quote with the sales team.

What is the possession date for SPACIA?

Srijan Realty advertises 2030 as the possession target. WBRERA records 31 December 2032 as the registered completion date. Confirm current timelines with Srijan Realty.

What amenities and shared facilities does SPACIA have?

Project facilities include an 18,000 sq.ft. clubhouse, 29,000+ sq.ft. of retail, landscaped spaces, a swimming pool, an outdoor gym, a butterfly garden and community hall. SPACIA is IGBC Platinum Pre-Certified.

What is nearby SPACIA?

The official brochure lists Reliance Mall at approximately 50 m, listed schools from approximately 200 m to 2.7 km, and hospitals from approximately 300 m to 2.3 km. It lists the upcoming New Barrackpore Metro Station at approximately 1.8 km, Madhyamgram Railway Station at 2 km and NSCBI Airport at 5.9 km; distances are approximate.

Are SPACIA apartments 3-side open?

Yes. SPACIA’s project details describe the apartments as 3-side open.
