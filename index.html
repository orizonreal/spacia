OVERVIEW

SPACIA by Srijan Realty at Madhyamgram

SPACIA brings premium 3 and 4 BHK residences to Jessore Road, beside Reliance Mall and close to Madhyamgram Chowmatha. The six G+12 towers sit across 3.6 acres, with three-side-open homes, generous open space, integrated retail and a connected community setting.

  • 3 and 4 BHK residences
  • Six G+12 towers across 3.6 acres

  • Three-side-open apartments with no shared walls
  • 18,000 sq.ft. clubhouse and 29,000+ sq.ft. of in-house retail
  • 20+ indoor and outdoor lifestyle amenities
  • Beside Reliance Mall on Jessore Road
  • Planned possession: 2030
